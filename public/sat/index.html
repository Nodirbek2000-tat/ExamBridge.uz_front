FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Digital SAT format?
The Digital SAT has two sections: Reading & Writing (54 questions, 64 minutes) and Math (44 questions, 70 minutes). Each section has two adaptive modules. Total score is 400–1600. Testing time is approximately 2 hours 14 minutes.
What is a good SAT score?
The national average is around 1060. A score of 1200+ is above average; 1400+ is excellent and competitive for most top universities. Ivy League and MIT typically see median scores of 1500–1580 from admitted students.
How many questions are on the SAT?
The Digital SAT has 98 scored questions: 54 in Reading & Writing and 44 in Math. Each section also has one unscored experimental module. Total questions seen during the test: approximately 108.
Is ExamBridge SAT practice free?
Yes, all SAT mock tests on ExamBridge are completely free. Create a free account to access full-length adaptive tests, detailed analytics, question explanations and your score history.
How is the SAT scored?
Each section (Reading & Writing, Math) is scored from 200 to 800. The total score is the sum of both section scores, ranging from 400 to 1600. There is no penalty for wrong answers — every question is worth attempting.
